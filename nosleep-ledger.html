Library

    ↗ Auto-grab from a Reddit post

    Two ways to skip retyping, both with no server and no CORS proxy:

    1 · Paste the link. When you paste a post URL into a new story, the title auto-fills from the link itself. Reliable, but it can't get the author.

    2 · Bookmarklet (also grabs the author, exact title, and the full story text for highlighting). Save this as a bookmark, then tap it while you're on the Reddit post — it opens the Ledger with a new story already filled in.

    🔖 Add to Ledger

    Drag that to your bookmarks bar on desktop. On iPhone: bookmark this page, then edit the bookmark and replace its address with the code below.

    Best-effort: if Reddit changes their page markup it may miss the author — the pasted-link title always works as a fallback.
